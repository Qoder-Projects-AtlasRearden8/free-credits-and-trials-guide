The rule that saves the most money

An alert tells you the house is on fire.
A hard cap is the sprinkler.

Most dashboards are good at sending email. That is not the same as stopping usage. Before you trust a guardrail, find out whether it blocks the request—and test it with a tiny limit.

Learn the difference
The two-wallet mistake
Credits

A dollar balance. It can run out, or the account can start charging your card.

Not a cap
Rate limits

They control how fast you can go, not the total monthly damage.

Not a cap
Alerts

They notify you after a threshold. Useful, but they do not stop the request.

Not a brake
Only a hard cap, quota, or tested block stops usage. Test yours.
