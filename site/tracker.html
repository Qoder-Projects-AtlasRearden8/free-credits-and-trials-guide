How to use it

A five-minute habit.

The tracker is deliberately boring. That is what makes it useful when the renewal email arrives.

  1. 01

    One row per offer

    Record the official URL, eligibility, card status, and the exact job you want it to do.

  2. 02

    Fill dates before the card

    Add the trial end, renewal date, and a decide-by date that leaves room to act.

  3. 03

    Set the native control

    Turn on the provider's spend cap or quota, then test it with a deliberately tiny limit.

  4. 04

    Review once a month

    Open the resource list, check the invoices, revoke unused keys, and close the row you finished.

Keep it useful, not sensitive.

Use a dedicated business email and an account identifier—never a password, full card number, recovery code, or API key. Do not put patient, student, or client information in the tracker. This template is recordkeeping, not a secret store.
