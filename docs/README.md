# The Free Tier Field Guide

**Free AI, cloud, and developer offers, with the catch included.** An Asdale Dynamics field guide for careful builders: plain English, dated facts, and a simple rule—never turn on a tool before you know how to turn it off.

This manual is for solo founders, authors, students, career changers, and small teams who want to use AI without mistaking a generous trial for a business model. It is written with a one-person Indiana LLC in mind, but the same questions apply to a nonprofit, a classroom, a side project, or a weekend experiment.

The companion site is in `site/`. The tracker template is [free-credit-tracker-template.csv](./free-credit-tracker-template.csv).

## The short version

1. There are different kinds of "free": ongoing free tiers, time-limited trial credits, startup or education programs, open-source software, and free chat products. Treat them differently.
2. An API free tier is not a budget. It is a rate limit with a marketing budget attached.
3. A cloud budget alert is usually a smoke alarm, not a sprinkler. Only a hard limit, quota, or automatic budget action actually stops spending.
4. The strongest long-term discount is portability: keep your prompts and data exportable, and avoid building your only workflow around one expiring credit.
5. Offers change. In 2026 alone, GitHub Models was retired, Cursor closed its student discount to new sign-ups, GitHub Copilot moved to usage-based billing, and AWS changed how its Free Tier works. Check the official page and write down the date.
6. Do not misrepresent yourself as a student, nonprofit, or venture-backed startup. The savings are not worth the account termination risk, and it is not the kind of company worth building.

## What this guide is not

- It is not a guaranteed list of active promotions. Programs can close, change amounts, impose regional limits, or alter data-use terms without notice.
- It is not financial, legal, tax, medical, or compliance advice. Formation, nonprofit status, taxes, contracts, and HIPAA compliance need a qualified professional and the provider's actual terms.
- It is not a recommendation to give any company your payment details. Use a business payment method you can monitor, and read the renewal terms.
- It does not contain affiliate links. No provider paid to be included.

## A note on sensitive data

Free tiers are often where privacy trade-offs live. Google's free Gemini API tier, for example, says content may be used to improve products. Mistral's free mode documentation notes data may be used for training unless you opt out. Terms vary by provider and change.

For a nurse, teacher, author, or adviser, that means: do not paste patient information, student records, client documents, unpublished manuscripts under NDA, or anything you would not put in a public forum into a free consumer AI tier. Use invented examples for learning. If you need protected health information workflows, you need a provider and contract that support that use, not merely a paid plan.

## How to use this manual with the tracker

The [CSV template](./free-credit-tracker-template.csv) has room for the offer, eligibility, dates, card status, renewal date, spend cap, cancellation URL, and notes. The two example rows are deliberately fake. Delete them and add your own.

A row is not finished until it has a **decide-by date** and a **cancel or downgrade URL**. That is the difference between a useful free trial and a future billing surprise.

## Freshness policy

Program facts in this edition were checked against official provider pages on **October 22, 2026**. The site displays the same date. Where a claim is uncertain, the text says so. Where an offer was closed or retired at the time of checking, it is described as such rather than quietly removed, because those changes are useful lessons.

If you maintain this guide, update the date in both the chapter front matter and the program data, rerun the build and tests, and review the change log.
